Math Pentathlon FIAR Rules

Objective

Be the first player to form a straight line of 4 chips in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) and click DECLARE FIAR!

1. Setup & Inventory

Each player receives 7 chips total (5 Regular Chips + 2 Fire Extinguisher FE Chips).

2. Placement Phase

Players alternate turns placing 1 chip onto any empty white circular spot on the board. Chips cannot be moved once placed until all 14 chips are on the board.

3. Movement Phase

Once all 14 chips are placed, players alternate sliding 1 chip along a connected line to an adjacent vacant spot.

4. Declaring FIAR

Victory is not automated! When you form 4 connected chips of your color in a straight line, click the DECLARE FIAR! button on your turn to win.
