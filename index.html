How to play

  • Players take turns placing 7 chips of their color on empty spaces. Two chips per color are marked ★.
  • After all 14 chips are placed, players take turns moving one chip along a straight connected line through empty spaces. No jumping or landing on an occupied space.
  • To win, identify 4 or more chips of one color on a straight connected line, with no opposite-color chip between them.
  • A winning line cannot cross the central yellow zone. A winning line also cannot use spaces directly connected by a line to an opponent's marked chip.
  • You may win using either color's chips. You must claim the line on your turn.
Prototype note: this is an independently drawn practice board and a first-pass rules implementation based on the public Division II tournament highlights. Its board geometry and movement network are simplified, not an official tournament board. Please compare with your physical board/rule manual before treating it as tournament-accurate.

How to move: Select one of your chips, then tap a green destination. During placement, tap any empty circle.
