Game setup

Math Pentathlon Division II FIAR • Not an official game • Practice edition • Developed by Kritesh

Who goes first?

Pick a hand. One hand holds the starting colour (Blue). Guess right and you play Blue and go first.

FIAR rules

Goal. Make 4 chips of one colour on a straight line of connected circles, with no chip of the opposite colour between them. The 4 chips can be yours or your opponent's. Empty circles between them are fine. The line cannot cross the fire in the centre.

Chips. Each player has 7 chips: 5 regular and 2 Fire Extinguishers (dotted). A Fire Extinguisher blocks chips of the opposite colour that touch it on a connected line, so they cannot count in a winning line. Fire Extinguishers can be part of a winning line. Blocked chips show a dashed ring.

Flag card. You act only while you hold the flag card. When you finish your turn you pass it to your opponent.

Phase I, placement. Blue starts. Take turns placing one chip on any empty circle until all 14 chips are down. No chip can be moved until every chip is placed, and a placed chip cannot be taken back. You can win during this phase.

Phase II, movement. Move one of your own chips any number of empty circles along one straight line. You cannot jump over chips or cross the fire.

Time limit. The whole game is timed. If the clock runs out before anyone claims FIAR, the game is a draw. You can change or turn off the limit in Game setup.

Claiming a win. You must claim FIAR while you still hold the flag card, before you pass it. If a player passes without claiming, the opponent can claim as soon as they receive the flag card. A wrong claim does nothing.
