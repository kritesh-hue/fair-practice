Game setup

Math Pentathlon Division II FIAR • Not an official game • Practice edition • Developed by Kritesh

Who goes first?

Pick a hand. One hand holds the starting colour (Green). Guess right and you go first.

FIAR rules

Goal. Make 4 or more chips of one colour in a straight line of connected circles, with no opponent chip in between. Empty circles between your chips are fine. The line cannot go through the fire in the centre.

Chips. Each player has 7 chips: 6 regular and 1 Fire Extinguisher (dotted). A Fire Extinguisher blocks every chip touching it (on a connected line), of either colour, from counting in a winning line. It never counts itself and cuts any line it sits on.

Phase I, placement. Take turns placing one chip on an empty circle until all 14 are down. You can win during this phase.

Phase II, movement. Move one chip any number of empty circles along one straight line. You cannot jump over chips or pass through the fire.

Ending. After you move, if a winning line exists, for either colour, press DECLARE FIAR! to win. If a player forgets, the opponent can declare on their turn. A wrong claim does nothing.
